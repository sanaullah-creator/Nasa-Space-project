# COSMIC LOST & FOUND — Demo Video Script (≈ 1 min 45 s)

**Format:** screen recording with voice-over. Desktop browser, 1440 px wide. Optionally cut to a phone view for 5 seconds near the end.
**Tip:** reset progress first (Achievements → Reset progress) so badges unlock live on camera.

| Time | On screen | Voice-over |
|---|---|---|
| **0:00–0:12** | Home page hero: glowing Mars, starfield, slow scroll to the stats bar | "Since 1966, NASA has left more than a dozen machines on the Moon, on Mars and in deep space. Some were left behind on purpose. Some wore out after heroic careers. And some are still talking to us. This is **COSMIC LOST & FOUND** — abandoned, but not forgotten." |
| **0:12–0:27** | Click **Explore Lost Missions** → type "voyager" → clear → click **Mars** → **Active** | "Our interactive museum holds fifteen researched mission profiles. Visitors can search, and filter by world — and, importantly, by honest status. Only one Mars machine is still active today: Perseverance." |
| **0:27–0:50** | Open **Opportunity** → scroll past the quick facts → stop at **Robot's Last Message**, let the typewriter run, click **Listen** | "Every mission page explains what the machine was, why NASA sent it, what it discovered and how it ended — with official NASA sources. Then the robot tells its own story. These messages are clearly labelled as creative storytelling — we even explain that Opportunity's famous 'my battery is low' line was never a real transmission." |
| **0:50–0:58** | "Badge unlocked: First Contact" toast appears | "Exploring earns badges, saved right in the browser." |
| **0:58–1:15** | **Space Map** → click the Apollo 15 marker (popup shows 3 machines) → **Mars** tab → zoom into Jezero → **Deep Space** tab → click Voyager 1 | "The interactive space map pins every landing site at its approximate real coordinates — like Hadley-Apennine, where Apollo 15 left its lander, rover and science station. In deep space, Voyager 1 is about to reach one light-day from Earth." |
| **1:15–1:28** | **Timeline** → Time Machine slider drag 1969 → 1997 → 2026; open one event | "The Mission Time Machine lets students travel through sixty years of exploration and see what was working — and what was already resting — in any year." |
| **1:28–1:38** | **Quiz** → answer one correctly (green + explanation) → quick cut to **Patch Lab** → "Surprise me" → Download PNG | "Then they test what they learned, and design their own mission patch to take home." |
| **1:38–1:45** | Back to home hero, logo centre | "COSMIC LOST & FOUND. Every machine has a mission. Every mission has a story." |

**Recording checklist:** close other tabs · 100% zoom · system voice installed for narration · hide bookmarks bar · record at 1080p or higher.
